Google Drive sync

Link your Drive with a Google popup. Files sync into a TONYDocs folder in your Drive as real .docx / .xlsx / .pptx files (plus your PDFs), so Drive previews them as actual documents. Each file carries the full TONYDocs data inside, so syncing restores it here exactly — edit in TONYDocs, view anywhere. The login stays on this device and reconnects automatically.

4 • Sync

Sync is live: edits push seconds after you stop typing, other devices pull in about every 90 seconds while the tab is open, and every revisit re-syncs automatically. Google sessions last about an hour — the app refreshes yours silently while it's open. If one lapses (e.g. overnight), press Sync now: one click reconnects and syncs.
